neural network for oll:
input: 21 * 6 length array of binary numbers
each 1 of 21 faces have 6 associated numbers corresponding to its potential color
output: probability distribution: 58 length array each corresponding to a different oll case
we have 1 solved state + 57 other cases

* we care about finding if the colors match the middle color => then we can identify the pattern

neural network for pll:
input: 21 * 6 length array of binary numbers
each 1 of 21 faces have 6 associated numbers corresponding to its potential color
output: probability distribution: 22 length array each corresponding to a different oll case
we have 1 solved state + 21 other cases

* we need to look at each of the 12 colors below the top layer (as this would be solved as one color).

Why have two separate neural networks for each of pll and oll? 
The network might get confused learning two separate concepts, since these two methods
need to be applied at two different stages of the cube it would be better, more faster to train both
separately.

Why have x, y, z rotations and not just have hard coded sides front, back, up, down, left, right?
The algorithms require them and that logic, (i.e. translating a 21 sticker color array representing the top layer to a 21 sticker array that represents the dominant color as the up face with the other colors translated into other faces.)
